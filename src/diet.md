# Daily Meal Plan

## 5:30 AM – Pre-Workout
- 15g whey protein isolate
- 3g creatine
- 1 banana
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 pinch of salt

## 7:00 AM – Breakfast
- 2 eggs
- 100g yogurt (100mg calcium, 3g protein, 60kcal)
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 10 cashew nuts
- 1 banana
- 1/2 orange
- 150g sweet potato
- Vitamin D3 + K2 (1000 IU)
- Omega-3 (900mg EPA + DHA)

## 8:30 AM
- 2g matcha powder (for L-theanine and flavonoids)

## 12:00 PM – Lunch
- 150g cooked rice
- 100g yogurt (100mg calcium, 3g protein, 60kcal)
- 1/2 orange (variants: 50g red bell pepper)
- 1 tbsp peanut butter
- 150g broccoli (variants: mustard greens / spinach / kale / amaranth greens / cabbage / potato)
- 150g beef (variants: lean pork / silkworm pupae / oyster / claim)

## 2:00 PM
- 10g 100% cocoa powder (for Cu and Flavonoid)

## 4:00 PM – Snack
- 1 banana
- 330ml milk (560mg calcium, 10g protein, 160kcal)
- 30g oats

## 7:00 PM – Dinner
- 150g cooked rice
- 100g yogurt (100mg calcium, 3g protein, 60kcal)
- 1/2 apple
- 150g choy sum (variants: Winter Melon / Malabar spinach / loofah / water spinach / pumpkin / potato)
- 150g chicken (variants: shrimp / grass carp / saba / salmon)

## 9:00 PM
- Zinc 5mg

## 10:00 PM
- Magnesium 200mg

## Notes
- All food weights are measured raw.
- Use iodized salt for cooking.
