# Highest impact on cognitive performance

## Sleep (7.5h at night and 30min nap, high quality and consistent) 
A bad night fails to bank the plasticity you earned by day, and it simultaneously raises tonic arousal, lowers dopamine receptor availability, and pushes your PFC offline. It's the one lever that, if missing, degrades **every other system at once**.

## Single-pointed focus / no multitasking
Acetylcholine raises signal-to-noise by sharpening one target and quieting the rest; with multiple targets the suppression system can't decide what to sharpen, so the mechanism breaks. This is the literal physical substrate of learning, which is why it ranks **just under sleep**.
