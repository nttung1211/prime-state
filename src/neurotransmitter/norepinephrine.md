# Norepinephrine (Noradrenaline)

> Note: The inverted-U and tonic/phasic locus-coeruleus picture is a robust,
> well-supported *model* of arousal and performance; the molecular and
> circuit detail is simplified. Confidence tags:
> [E] established · [R] reasonably supported · [M] model / extrapolation.

- Norepinephrine is the global *alertness / arousal / engagement* dial —
  NOT "the stress chemical." Too little = drowsy, flat, apathetic. It is
  to be tuned, not minimized. [R]
- It's made in one tiny brainstem nucleus (locus coeruleus) wired to
  almost the whole brain. It doesn't carry content; it sets *gain* — how
  responsively the entire system operates. [E]
- It is the power behind acetylcholine's spotlight: perfect focus with no
  arousal behind it illuminates nothing. The two multiply each other. [R]
- Moderate arousal *enhances* plasticity and tags arousing events for
  strong memory; sustained excess *impairs* it, especially the
  prefrontal cortex. [R]
- Core principle — the inverted-U: performance rises with arousal to a
  peak, then *declines*. More is not better past the peak. [E]
- Why the right side collapses: past a threshold, the prefrontal cortex
  (planning, focus, impulse control — the seat of deep work) goes
  effectively *offline*; control shifts to reflexive/habitual circuits.
  "Try harder" raises arousal → pushes PFC further off. [R]
- Optimal = **low tonic + crisp phasic** (the midpoint of the U shape) - calm but sharp.
  Over-peak = **high tonic + blurred phasic** (the right side of the U shape) — and high tonic *is* the felt sensation of
  anxious, restless, can't-settle scanning. 
  Too low tonic = drowsy, flat, apathetic. [M]
- Sensation to read: "Am I UNDER, AT, or OVER the peak?" This diagnosis
  is the master skill — the correct fix is *opposite* on each side. [M]

# How to Tune It

- Diagnose first, every time. UNDER = foggy/flat/bored/no urgency. AT =
  alert+calm, absorbed, traction, time distorts. OVER = restless,
  darting attention, urgency without output, re-reading. [M reasoning]
- RAISE it (when under): brief intense movement, cold exposure, a *real*
  near-term stake, novelty / change of environment, sharper breathing,
  and above all *just start* (action itself raises arousal). [R]
- LOWER it (when over): extended exhale > inhale; physiological sigh
  (double inhale, long exhale ×1–3) drops arousal in ~30s; cut stimulus
  and stakes; narrow to one concrete next action; and *just start* —
  anticipation usually carries more arousal than the doing. [R]
- Note: *starting* is the correct move whether under OR over the peak —
  opposite mechanisms, same action. When in doubt, begin. [M reasoning]
- Caffeine = a directional tool, not a default. It climbs you *up* the
  curve: great if under the peak, counterproductive if already over
  (deepens jittery/scattered). Resolves why coffee sometimes helps focus
  and sometimes wrecks it — depends which side you were on. [R reasoning]
- Oscillate; never sustain high arousal. Acute spike + true recovery =
  adaptive (trains the system, like the gym). Chronic unrecovered high
  tonic = wrecks all three systems at once (PFC, plasticity, dopamine
  baseline). Recovery breaks must be *low* stimulation. [R]
- Foundation: under-sleep *raises* tonic arousal while *impairing* the
  PFC — wired + impaired, parked over the peak before any choice. [R]
  Trained exercise *widens and stabilizes* the usable range — fitness
  literally broadens the top of your U. [R]
  Protein → tyrosine is the precursor of norepinephrine too. [E synthesis]
