# Habits
Exercise, sleep, real food, sunlight, stress recovery, real social connection and deep work are the pillars of a prime state.

## Get Morning Sunlight

**Description**

Expose your eyes to natural sunlight for 10–15 minutes shortly after waking up, ideally during sunrise or early morning.

**Purpose**

Reset your circadian rhythm, boost morning alertness, and promote melatonin production 14–16 hours later for better sleep at night.

**Timing**

Within 1 hour after waking up, every day.

---

## Get Daily Sun Exposure for Vitamin D

**Description**

Spend at least 20 minutes outdoors each day to get natural sunlight to support vitamin D production.

**Purpose**

Support vitamin D synthesis, which is essential for bone health, immune function, testosterone production, and overall well-being.

**Timing**

10 minutes around 8:00 AM and 10 minutes around 4:00 PM.

---

## Maintain a Consistent Sleep Schedule

**Description**

Sleep from 10:00 PM to 5:30 AM every night, and avoid blue light exposure (from phones, computers, and TVs) starting at 9:30 PM.

**Purpose**

High-quality sleep improves testosterone and growth hormone secretion, enhances memory and focus, strengthens the immune system, and helps maintain stable energy, mood, and appetite throughout the day.
